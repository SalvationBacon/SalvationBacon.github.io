> FAIR QUESTIONS
Isn't AI-made stuff low effort?

The code comes fast. The game doesn't. Every game here is shaped by hundreds of my decisions: what to build, what feels off, what to cut, what to try again.

8-Bit Industry has had over 100 updates since March, each one from me playing it and asking for changes.

Was the art made with an image generator?

No. There are no image generators and no image or sound files in these games at all. Every sprite is drawn pixel by pixel by code Claude wrote, and the music and sound effects are played live in your browser by code too.

Why are they free?

Because I made them for fun, not money. No ads, no account, no store. If you enjoy one, the best thanks is telling a friend. If you'd like to chip in anyway, there's a Ko-fi, but it's never needed.

Why tell everyone it's AI-made?

Because it's true, and you deserve to know what you're playing. Plenty of people don't like AI, and that's fair. I think it can let people who aren't programmers make the games they've always imagined, and I'd rather be honest about it.

Can I give feedback or report a bug?

Yes please. Feedback is what makes these games better. Every game has its own page on my GitHub, where you can open an issue.
